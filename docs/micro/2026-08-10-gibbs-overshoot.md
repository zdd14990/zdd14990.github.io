Adding more Fourier terms makes a square-wave approximation sharper, but the overshoot near a jump does not disappear. It narrows toward the discontinuity while remaining about nine percent of the jump—the **Gibbs phenomenon**.

The new [Fourier visualizer](/lab/fourier/) makes that much easier to see than a static plot.
